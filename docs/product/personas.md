# Personas

## Authentication developer

Needs to configure an OAuth/OIDC or federation flow, exercise a test issuer or
relying party, inspect each exchange, diagnose validation failures, and retain
a redacted reproducible report.

## Security engineer or tester

Needs protocol-aware checks, negative test cases, controlled mutations, and
clear evidence that a weakness reproduces only against an authorized test
target or the platform's isolated vulnerable fixture.

## Learner

Needs a safe, understandable path from protocol concepts to visible messages,
security decisions, attack outcomes, mitigations, and verification.

## Protocol implementer or maintainer

Needs role- and profile-based conformance tests, deterministic test fixtures,
and collection/report formats that can be repeated locally or in CI.

## Educator or team lead

Needs versioned, resettable exercises and shareable collections that do not
contain reusable secrets or depend on a hosted identity platform.
