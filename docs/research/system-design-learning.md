# System-Design Learning Notes

Progressively study identity, credentials, claims, sessions, cookies, tokens,
revocation, availability, consistency, replication, caching, queues,
idempotency, retries, timeouts, failure domains, threat modeling,
cryptography, PKI, OAuth/OIDC, SAML, LDAP, and Kerberos.

For every major component, document its problem, inputs and outputs, owned
state, failure behavior, scaling model, consistency needs, trust boundary,
observability, audit behavior, and rejected alternatives.
