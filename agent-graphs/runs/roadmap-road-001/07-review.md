# D7 — Skeptic review

**Purpose:** Challenge ROAD-001's session guide independently of implementation.

**Inputs:** D1–D6, `docs/research/user-task-validation.md`, ROAD-001/002.

## Findings

1. **The guide could test protocol knowledge more than usability.** Mitigation: prompts ask participants to use screen evidence and state what remains unknown; scorecards separate task outcome, observed action, and interpretation.
2. **Prompts could reveal expected terminology and bias outcomes.** Mitigation: the facilitator must not name state/PKCE before initial interpretation; recovery prompts are neutral and every prompt is recorded.
3. **A five-person sample cannot establish demand or compare all protocols.** Mitigation: the guide calls results directional, uses protocol task rotation only for coverage, and forbids statistical/general market claims.
4. **Sensitive participant material could enter notes.** Mitigation: synthetic examples only, participant codes, no real credentials/customer traces, no recording without separate agreement, and minimal retained data.
5. **Decision thresholds might be treated as scientific cutoffs.** Mitigation: they are explicitly draft, owner-reviewed before sessions, and paired with revise/pause/inconclusive outcomes and qualitative evidence.
6. **Guide may overstate completion.** Mitigation: ROAD-001 status is “drafted; product-owner review and threshold signoff are pending”; ROAD-002 sessions remain undone.

No blocking findings. One coverage improvement was made: across the five sessions, include at least one JWT, one HTTP, and one SAML task, while stating that this is not a comparative benchmark.
